How to Play

1. Explore & Connect

Rotate the globe and tap an airport (white dot). Tap 'OPEN NEW ROUTE', then select a nearby airport (gold dot) to connect them.

2. Network Bonus

Planes earn fixed income based on your total network size. Expanding your routes steadily increases your overall profit multiplier.

3. Buy Planes

Use the green Fleet button to buy planes. Only active planes (assigned to routes) will generate income and carry passengers.

4. Rivals

Other colored lines are rival airlines expanding. Currently, they are harmless, but stay tuned for future competition updates!
